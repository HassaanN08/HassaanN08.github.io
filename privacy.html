Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

ThinkPad Backup is a personal-use application operated solely by its owner.

It uses Rclone to access files it creates in the owner's Google Drive, for the purpose of storing and retrieving personal backups.

Backup contents are encrypted locally using Restic before being uploaded to Google Drive.

The application does not operate a separate server, sell personal data, or share Google account information with third parties.

Google Drive access can be revoked through the owner's Google Account settings.
